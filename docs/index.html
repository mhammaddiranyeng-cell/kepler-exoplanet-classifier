RiverExtract

Senior Design

AUST · Advised by Dr. Gaby Abou Haidar · Co-advised by Dr. Chafic Saide

A stationary, conveyor-based river waste collection and sorting system — built as a working prototype and specified as a full-scale, solar-powered installation.

Engineering detail

Tiny Neural Predictors in DPCM

IEEE Paper

Cross-domain study — speech & ECG · 8-page IEEEtran

“Tiny Neural Network Predictors in DPCM: A Cross-Domain Study on Speech and ECG with Closed-Loop Stability and Evaluation Pitfalls.” A complete DPCM/ADPCM compression pipeline in Python/PyTorch.

Engineering detail
  • Five tiny neural predictor configurations (≤1500 parameters each) benchmarked against a well-tuned linear baseline.
  • Two domains: the Arabic Speech Commands Dataset and the MIT-BIH Arrhythmia (ECG) Database.
  • Closed-loop stability analysis and a catalogue of evaluation pitfalls that inflate reported gains.
  • Finding: no tested tiny NN robustly beats the linear baseline under fair multi-slice evaluation — an honest negative result kept in the final paper.
  • PyTorch
  • DSP
  • DPCM/ADPCM
  • ECG

IoT Water Pump Controller

Built

ESP32 + HiveMQ Cloud MQTT

A “manual-start, auto-stop” pump controller extending the RiverExtract IoT architecture — remote triggering with a hardware-safe stop condition.

Engineering detail
  • Latch logic: PumpRun = (RemoteON_cmd OR PumpRun) AND NOT FloatFull.
